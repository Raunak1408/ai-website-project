Care Guide

Coleus plants thrive in partial shade to full sun, depending on the variety. Most prefer bright, indirect light for vivid foliage colors.

Keep the soil consistently moist but not soggy. Water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Avoid letting plants sit in water.

Use well-draining potting soil rich in organic matter. Fertilize monthly during the growing season with a balanced fertilizer.

Propagate Coleus easily via stem cuttings; root cuttings in water or moist soil until roots form.
