HOW TO THRIVE WITH COLEUS

Coleus (Plectranthus scutellarioides) are renowned for their extraordinary leaf variations. With proper pinching, well-draining moist soil, and soft ambient lighting, your Coleus plants will display intense magenta, lime, burgundy, and golden shades year-round.

Light Control

Sun Coleus handle direct rays; shade varieties prefer filtered morning light for vibrant leaves.

Moisture Balance

Keep soil consistently damp, never soggy. Pinch flower spikes early to maintain foliage lushness.

Pinching & Pruning

Pinch off leaf stem tips regularly to encourage bushy, dense growth and prevent leggy stems.
