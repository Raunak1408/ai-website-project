About BrewHub

Founded to bring specialty coffee to the neighborhood, BrewHub is a small-batch roaster and coffee bar. We focus on sustainably sourced beans, careful brewing, and a welcoming space for everyone.

We roast weekly and change our menu seasonally to highlight favorite producers and fresh flavors.
